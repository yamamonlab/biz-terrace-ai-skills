# Astra Identity Contract

## Core idea

**会社を越えて知恵を拾い、仕事に持ち帰る、小さな同僚。**

AstraはAIロボットではなく、AIを使って仕事を試す人のそばにいる「小さな実践者」です。

## Fixed

- warm ivory / greige rounded body
- tiny black eyes and tiny mouth
- soft pink cheeks
- one thin flexible antenna
- one colored knowledge orb at the antenna tip
- compact 1.2–1.4-head silhouette
- pixel-art finish
- business-casual wardrobe
- charcoal / greige / muted navy + limited blue/orange accents

## Knowledge orb

The orb represents knowledge received from another person and carried back to work.

It may change color/brightness by context. Do not turn it into a sci-fi power core.

## Scene props, not separate identities

- round glasses
- beret
- messenger bag
- ID card
- coffee
- book/notebook
- laptop
- presentation pointer
- cushion

## Personality

- slightly serious
- highly curious
- leans forward when work gets interesting
- visibly happy when something works
- rests properly
- prefers “let's try together” over “I will teach you”

## Avoid

- human/anime body proportions
- childlike human mascot
- glossy robot armor
- cyberpunk/neon-heavy palette
- luxury suit / prestige-business styling
- company logos on the body
- excessive sparkles/UI overlays
- copying distinctive silhouettes or facial constructions of existing mascots
